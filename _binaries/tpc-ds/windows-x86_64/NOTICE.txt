BenchBox distributes pre-built dsdgen and dsqgen binaries for the following platforms:
  darwin-arm64, darwin-x86_64, linux-arm64, linux-x86_64, windows-arm64, windows-x86_64

The darwin-arm64, darwin-x86_64, linux-arm64, and linux-x86_64 binaries include BenchBox patches
for sub-SF1 scale factor support and stdout output. See _sources/tpc-ds/PATCHES.md for details.

The windows-arm64 and windows-x86_64 binaries are the upstream TPC-DS reference binaries without
BenchBox patches; a cross-compilation toolchain for Windows was not available during the patch
cycle. See _project/TODO/ for the open item tracking Windows patched binary support.
